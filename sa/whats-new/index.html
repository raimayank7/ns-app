किं नूतनम्

App मध्ये दृश्यमानं प्रत्येकं परिवर्तनं, नूतनतमं प्रथमम्। App स्वयम् अद्यतनं भवति: नूतनसंस्करणार्थं पुनः उद्घाटयतु।

अन्तिमम् अद्यतनम्: 19 नवम्बर 2026

नवम्बर 2026

19 नवम्बर 2026

  • Works like an accounting program Menus and screens now use accounting names (Sales Voucher, Receipt Voucher, Sundry Debtors, Bills Outstanding), and one Masters screen keeps ledgers, groups, banks, stock items, units, transporters and vehicles.
  • Set money against bills Receipt and Payment vouchers with any ledger, Adjust bills, opening balances as separate old bills, interest on late payment and credit limits that stop a bill.
  • Your own fields and voucher types Add custom fields to parties, items and vouchers; set voucher types with their own number series; a Narration box on every voucher.
  • Orders, payroll and TDS Orders and pending goods, receipt notes and returns, freight added to the goods' cost, a salary run with PF and ESI dates, and TDS returns with a file for the return.
  • Safer books Cancel a voucher and keep its number (it prints CANCELLED), an edit history of every change, and no App screen prints blank pages any more. Help has real pictures of the screens.
  • Export invoices count Export and SEZ invoices from the export invoice maker now reach GSTR-1, GSTR-3B 3.1(b) and your accounts as sales. Earlier ones move over by themselves.
  • GST to the paisa CGST and SGST are split the same way on the bill, in the books, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and the e-invoice. GSTR-2B credit notes reduce your ITC, and an advance from another state gets IGST.
  • Safer bill numbers A double click saves once, a number typed twice is refused, and the recycle bin never brings back a number that is already used again.
  • Accounts fixes Sold fixed assets leave the books, PF, ESI and professional tax can be paid with Pay tax, advances on hotel, job card and bilty bills reach the books, and the day book shows names, not codes.
  • Easier to use Every Alt+G code works, a sale bill opens as a sale bill when you edit it, the credit line sits at the bottom of every printed page, the browser's own autofill stays out of the way, and the new screens are in all 23 languages.

18 नवम्बर 2026

  • New NSBiz logo The App and the Windows program have a new icon: the NS emblem with the gold BIZ pill. The loading screen shows it too, and the Dashboard is now clean with only the logo in the middle.
  • Screens like software Smaller, tidier inner screens without repeated buttons. The party screen keeps Preview Party Ledger, Print, Share PDF, Excel and Send on WhatsApp at the top with its other buttons.
  • Journal entry like a ledger page Journal entries are typed in a grid: account, name, Dr and Cr, with the totals and any difference right under the columns, and the narration below.
  • Esc goes back Esc on any screen, such as the Trial Balance, goes back one step, like Tally. New party and New item on the Dashboard open the quick add box right there.
  • Fixes from user testing A party or item added with F2 now fills the open bill and is saved once, bill numbers are checked again when you save, each bill line has a Disc. % column, GST in the books matches the printed invoice to the paisa, Alt+G finds the new reports, and the menus are in Urdu too.
  • Voucher lines in a grid Bills are entered in a grid with columns for item, HSN, quantity, UOM (unit), rate, discount, GST and amount.
  • Ship to and dispatch details A sale bill can have a separate Ship to address and Dispatch details (dispatched through, destination, transport mode, vehicle no., LR no. and date, e-way bill no.), printed on the bill.
  • E-invoice and e-way bill files checked The e-invoice and e-way bill JSON files were checked against the official formats of the portals, and the transporter name now keeps to the portal's 25-character limit.
  • Printing fixed Reports and party ledgers print properly again, with only the NSBiz credit line at the bottom.
  • Easier on the eyes in dark mode Reports such as the Chart of Accounts now use soft dark colours in dark mode. Printouts and PDFs stay white.
  • Debtors and creditors lists Reports has a Debtors list and a Creditors list as on any date: phone, GSTIN, city, oldest bill, days overdue, credit limit or MSME size, with Print, PDF and Excel.
  • Sales and purchase summaries Totals by month, party, item or HSN. Click a month for its days and a day for its bills.
  • Voucher registers and cash and bank summary Receipt, payment, journal, contra, credit note and debit note registers, and cash and bank month by month.
  • Chart of Accounts and any ledger Masters has a Chart of Accounts with every account under its group. Click any account, also in the Trial Balance, to open its ledger.
  • Print many vouchers Utilities > Print many vouchers prints every invoice, receipt or other document of a period one after another.
  • One credit line on documents The bottom of every document now carries just NSBiz by Nivesh Sahayak.
  • Menus like accounting software The side menu is now arranged by accounting job: Masters, Transactions, Reports, GST and tax, Utilities, with the voucher entries, Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss and Trial Balance in it. On a computer a menu bar (Masters, Transactions, Reports...) and a voucher toolbar (Sale F8, Purchase F9, Receipt F6...) sit at the top, and the Dashboard has a Shortcuts panel on the right.
  • Quick entry your way Customize in Quick entry chooses which tabs show and their order, the GST % of a new line, what Money received now and Paid now start with, and round off.
  • Every document with a bottom bar Invoices, quotations, challans and all the other documents now keep New, Saved, Save, Print and Share in one bar at the bottom, like the entry screens.
  • Document list A to Z Make a new document and More free tools are listed in A to Z order.
  • The bell, search and menu work during an entry While an entry screen is open (Quick entry, a bill, money received...) the bell, search, Help, the side menu and the status bar can still be used; Esc goes back.
  • Documents inside the App look like entries Invoices and the other documents opened inside the App now have one bar at the bottom (New, Saved, Save, Print, Share) like the App's entry screens; Esc goes back.
  • Windows program 0.2.3 The installer now closes NSBiz first if it is open, so the update installs cleanly.
  • Add a new party or item while making a voucher Type a name that is not saved yet and a line under the box offers Add; or press F2 in the box. A full screen asks the details (group, GSTIN, phone; more details like PAN, credit days, opening balance, opening stock are optional), saves it to your books and fills the voucher.
  • Accounting group names The built-in party groups are now called Sundry Debtors and Sundry Creditors, and a new party's ledger group can be chosen when it is made.
  • Work on two things at once Open another screen (a party ledger from Ctrl K, a report, an Alt key) while an entry is half typed: the entry is kept open in the status bar under Open entries with everything you typed. Click it to carry on where you stopped.
  • Open any entry Every entry in a list now has an Open button (eye): see its items, the accounting entry (debit and credit) and the bill photo, then Edit or Print. List buttons are now small icons; hover to see their name.
  • Compact screens On computers the App, My data and the document makers use smaller text and tighter buttons like desktop software, so more fits on the screen. Phones keep the larger size.
  • No repeats in the side menu Inside the App the document makers sit under Make a new document, and tools that are already App screens (Cash book, Cheques, Khata and others) are no longer listed twice.
  • What you sold to a party On a party's page press Items to see every item sold to them (and bought from them) in the dates you pick: quantity, average rate, bills and amount, with Excel.
  • Lists look like software Lists of bills and entries are now tight rows with lines between them, and the party page keeps its buttons, dates and filters in one bar.
  • Notifications bell A bell at the top shows how many things need you today; it opens the list (dues, bills to pay, cheques, tax dates, low stock) and each line opens its screen. The number no longer sits on the taskbar icon.
  • Arrange your Dashboard Press Customize beside Dashboard to choose which Quick add buttons, money tiles and cards you see, and their order. Sale bill, Contra and Purchase return can be added to Quick add.
  • Every tool opens full screen The boxes inside tools (hotel rooms, garage jobs, dairy, rent and others) now open as full screens too, and the shortcuts list and page print properly.
  • Entries open as full screens Sale bills, purchase bills, money received and paid, expenses, journals, production and the other entries now open as a full screen beside the menu, like desktop accounting software: Enter for the next box, Ctrl+S to save, Esc to go back, and a check before leaving with unsaved typing.
  • The App is now called NSBiz Same App, same data, new short name: NSBiz by Nivesh Sahayak, also for the Windows program.
  • Everything stays inside NSBiz Help, What's new, My data, your settings and our other pages now open on an NSBiz screen with Back, so the menu and the Dashboard are always one click away.
  • Only the tools of your business The type of business you pick hides the other tools at once, even before you fill in your business details, and the search and the keys leave them out too.
  • Keyboard shortcuts page Every key explained on one page, ready to print: open it from the list of keys (?).
  • Shortcuts for every tool Tally-style keys: F8 sale, F9 purchase, F6 receipt, F5 payment, F4 contra, F7 journal, F10 expense. Alt keys open the main screens, and Alt+G with a 2-letter code opens any screen or document. Press ? for the full list.
  • Windows-निमित्तं Nivesh Sahayak सम्पूर्णम् App अन्तः धारयत् निःशुल्कं Windows-प्रोग्राम: कुतः अपि Ctrl+Alt+N द्वारा त्वरित-प्रविष्टिः, पुनः पुनः अपृष्ट्वा फोल्डर-मध्ये बैकअप, Excel-निमित्तं डेटाबेस-प्रतिलिपिः च। मेनू-स्थात् Windows-प्रोग्राम-पृष्ठात् अवतारयतु।

17 नवम्बर 2026

  • Party ledger, your way Switch party from the ledger screen, see the entries first and open the ledger page only when you press Preview Party Ledger. Each party remembers the tab and dates you used last.
  • Dues follow-up See when each customer was last reminded, note the date they promised to pay, and send WhatsApp reminders to several customers one by one.
  • WhatsApp on a bill Every sale in the entries list has a WhatsApp button with the bill number, amount and what is still due.
  • Money this month The home screen now shows money in, money out and the net for the month, and your top 5 customers who owe you.
  • Everything opens inside the App Links between tools and pages (guides, other tools, the Accountant viewer) now open on an App screen with Back working, instead of a new tab or a page inside a page.
  • MSME payables Mark suppliers micro or small and see which bills are past the legal payment limit, with a 31 March list of unpaid bills whose tax deduction is at risk (Excel and print).
  • Limits helper See how your own sales stand against the composition, e-invoice and e-way bill limits, with links to the official notifications.
  • Backup health My day shows how many days ago you saved a backup and whether the auto-save folder is on, with a Back up now button.
  • MSME payables See which small and micro suppliers you owe beyond the legal payment time, with a year-end report for Excel and print. Rules shown as of 17 November 2026, so check the current notification.
  • Limits helper A quick check of the GST limits that matter to you: composition, e-invoice and e-way bill.
  • Backup health A card that tells you when your last backup was and whether your data is safely saved.
  • Shared folder (try it, off by default) Use one shared folder on several computers, for example a Windows share or OneDrive folder, so everyone sees the same bills. Switch it on in My data. Works in Chrome and Edge on computers.
  • Send the PDF on WhatsApp from a computer After saving the PDF, a bar opens the customer's WhatsApp chat with the message ready, and tells you to attach the saved file. On phones the share sheet already carries the PDF.
  • Weights on bills Type 250g, 1kg+500g or 2 ltr on a purchase bill line and it is worked out in kg or litres, with the rate and amount. Route and beat fields and a route-wise dues report help distributors.
  • Milk Diary and Subscribers Two new tools: a milk collection and delivery diary with farmer and customer passbooks, and monthly billing for cable, internet, tiffin and water-can subscribers.
  • Trips and Trip Sheets Bilty now has a trips and fleet desk with profit per vehicle, driver cash, freight dues and vehicle paper reminders. A new Trip Sheets tool serves taxi, travel agency and courier operators.
  • Donations and warranty Donation receipts with a donor and member ledger for trusts, NGOs and gurdwaras, and a warranty and service-due screen for electronics and appliance sellers.
  • Bring your data from other software In My data, bring parties, items and opening balances from Vyapar, Tally, Busy or an Excel sheet, check them first, and undo the import in one tap.
  • Quick entry Type sale bills, purchase bills, money received, money paid and expenses one after another by keyboard: press Alt+Q, Enter moves to the next box and Ctrl+S saves and opens the next blank one.
  • Only the tools for your business The App now shows only the tools that suit your type of business, in any browser as well as the installed App. Turn others on any time in Settings > Additional features.
  • Declarations in the official layouts Forms 31, 97, 121 to 128 and 184 now print in the Income Tax Department's own layout, ready to hand over.
