What the app does for a small business

Most free bill makers stop at the bill. The Nivesh Sahayak App keeps going: it links every payment to the bills it settles, so you always know which customer still owes how much and for how long, and whom you still have to pay.

  • Dashboard: today's and this month's sales, money to receive and to pay, cash in hand and money in the bank.
  • Money in and out: record a payment once and tick the bills it settles. Part payments and advances are handled for you, and TDS cut by a customer is counted.
  • Dues with ageing: who owes you, split into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days, with a ready WhatsApp reminder.
  • Party statement: a statement of account for any dates, to print or share as a PDF.
  • Purchases and expenses: supplier bills with CGST, SGST or IGST worked out, purchase returns, and everyday expenses such as rent, electricity and fuel.
  • Cash and bank books: every cash and bank entry with a running balance.
  • One-click convert: a quotation becomes a sales order, delivery challan or invoice without typing it again.
